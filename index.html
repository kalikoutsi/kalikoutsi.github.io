How we work

Three plain promises

  1. Your data stays on your device

    What you put into our apps is saved on your phone. We do not collect it, so we cannot see it.

  2. No accounts, no ads, no tracking

    You do not sign up to use our apps. There are no adverts, and nothing watches what you do.

  3. We say plainly what an app does

    Each app page says what the app does, what it does not do, and where your data goes.
