Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy covers Kalikoutsi, this website, and the principles every one of our apps is built on. It's built so that your data belongs to you alone. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what that means.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. Our apps do not require an account or sign-up of any kind. Your workouts, exercise history, body metrics, program selections, and any other information you enter are stored only on your device. We have no server that receives this data, and we do not see it, store it, or have any way to access it.

Analytics and tracking

Our apps contain no analytics software development kits, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking pixels. We do not know how many people use our apps, how they use them, or who they are.

Opt-in crash reporting

Our apps may include optional crash reporting, to help us fix faults. Where it exists it is off by default and only ever active if you explicitly turn it on. A crash report sends a stack trace, your device model, and the OS and app version to Sentry, hosted in the EU — nothing that identifies you, and none of the data you have entered. You can turn it off again at any time.

Each app has its own policy

This page is the policy for Kalikoutsi and this website, and sets the standard every one of our apps is held to. Each app also publishes a policy of its own describing exactly what that app does, and where an app's policy differs from this one, the app's policy governs.

  • GymTracker privacy policy

Data sharing

Since we don't collect your workout or body data, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or disclose to third parties. The only third-party service involved anywhere in our apps is the opt-in crash reporting described above, and only if you turn it on.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age, beyond the opt-in crash reports described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of our apps after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@kalikoutsi.com.
