Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Paltheon is built so that your training data belongs to you alone. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. Paltheon does not require an account or sign-up of any kind. Your workouts, exercise history, body metrics, program selections, and any other information you enter into the app are stored only on your device. We have no server that receives this data, and we do not see it, store it, or have any way to access it.

Analytics and tracking

Paltheon contains no analytics software development kits, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking pixels. We do not know how many people use the app, how they use it, or who they are.

Future opt-in crash reporting

A future version of Paltheon may offer crash reporting to help us fix bugs. If introduced, this feature will be off by default and only ever active if you explicitly turn it on in Settings. Crash reports, if enabled, will not contain personal identifiers, workout data, or body metrics — only the technical information needed to diagnose the crash itself.

Data sharing

Since Paltheon does not collect your data, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or disclose to third parties. There are no third-party services integrated into the app that receive your information.

Children's privacy

Paltheon is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Paltheon after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@paltheon.com.
