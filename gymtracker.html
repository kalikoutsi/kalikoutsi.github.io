At a glance

What it is
An app to log your gym workouts, plan your training and see how it adds up.
Who it is for
Anyone who lifts weights, does cardio, or both. At a gym, at home or on the road. New or experienced.
Where your data lives
On your phone. There is no account and no server.
What it does not do
No ads. No tracking. No social feed. No food diary. It does not give medical advice.
What it costs
Pricing will be announced before launch.

Where your data is stored

On your phone. Nothing leaves it unless you connect, switch on or send something yourself.

  • Stays on your phone

    Your training log

    Every workout, set, weigh-in, goal and setting is saved on your phone. There is no account and no server. We cannot see your data, and we never have a copy.

  • Only if you connect it

    Apple Health

    Apple Health is off until you connect it. Then the app can read your weight, a few body details and cardio from other apps. It adds your finished workouts and weigh-ins to Health. You choose what it may read, and you can disconnect at any time.

  • Only if you turn it on

    Crash reports

    Crash reports are off unless you switch them on in Settings. If you do, a crash sends a technical report to Sentry, a crash service hosted in the EU. It holds your device model and app version. It never holds your name, your workouts or your Health data.

  • Only if you send it

    Backups and spreadsheets

    The app saves backups on your phone. You can also make a spreadsheet of your log. A file only leaves your phone when you send it yourself, for example by email or AirDrop.

There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking in the app. Reminders are set on the phone itself, so we never know when one goes off. The full detail is in the GymTracker privacy policy.

What it does

Seven groups. Open “In detail” under any of them for the full list.

  • Log a workout

    Tick off each set as you go. The app keeps count.

    In detail
    • Each set records the weight and the reps. You can also rate how hard it felt, from 5 to 10.
    • Ticking a set starts a rest timer. It aims for 90 seconds on big lifts and 60 on single-muscle moves. You can change it.
    • Mark a set as a warm-up. It stays in your log but never counts toward records.
    • For runs, rides, rows, swims and walks, log the time and distance. The app shows your pace.
    • Nothing planned today? Quick Start begins any routine.
    • Your workout shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. On iOS 17 or later you can log the set, skip a rest or add 30 seconds from there.
    • If you like, the app can say out loud when your rest is over.

  • Plan your weeks

    Set a goal or pick a program, and your calendar fills in.

    In detail
    • A goal says what you are working towards, and by when. The app picks a program to match.
    • You choose the days you can train. Sessions only go on those days.
    • Tell the app what equipment you have, or pick “just my body”. It only suggests what you can do.
    • Missing a piece of kit today? Swap the exercise for one that works the same muscles.
    • Tell it which areas are sore or injured. It stops suggesting them, but never stops you.
    • The calendar marks each day: ✓ done, ✕ missed, ✚ sick day, or skipped.
    • A short weekly check-in asks how it is going. Nothing changes unless you choose.
    • A weight goal shows a rough daily calorie figure. It is an estimate, not advice, and there is no food diary.

  • Find exercises

    413 exercises, each with a short demo video.

    In detail
    • Every built-in exercise has a looping demo clip. The clips are inside the app, so they play without internet.
    • A body figure shows which muscles each exercise works.
    • Each exercise is marked Compound, Isolation or Cardio.
    • Search the Library for exercises, routines and programs in one box.
    • Use the built-in routines and programs, or copy one and make it your own.
    • Add your own exercises and routines.
    • Share a routine you made with a friend as a small file. It holds the routine and nothing personal.

  • See your progress

    Charts of the work you have done.

    In detail
    • Last 7 days: one bar for each day’s work. Drag it to look further back.
    • Muscle effort: a front and back body figure. Each muscle is coloured by how much work it got over the last 4 weeks.
    • Weight chart: your weigh-ins on a timeline, with the healthy range for your height.
    • Personal records: your heaviest weight, most reps, and estimated one-rep max. For cardio, your longest distance and fastest pace.
    • Streaks count the weeks you trained without missing a planned workout. A sick day freezes a streak instead of breaking it.
    • Awards: medals and trophies. Once you earn one, it is never taken back.
    • A training profile with six readings on one chart.

  • Your companion

    Pick one of eleven companions. It grows as you keep training, never from your weight.

    Cinder at each of the six levels

    1. Level 1When you pick it
    2. Level 2First good week
    3. Level 34 good weeks
    4. Level 410 good weeks
    5. Level 520 good weeks
    6. Level 648 good weeks

    A good week is a week with workouts on at least two different days. The weeks do not have to be in a row.

    The eleven companions at level 6

    • Rivet
    • Cinder
    • Grit
    • Bram
    • Rin
    • Oren
    • Lumi
    • Kael
    • Pip
    • Elowen
    • Ash
    In detail
    • There are eleven companions, from a scrap-can robot to a flower fairy and a wolf pup. Each has its own voice.
    • Level 2 also comes after three workout days within any seven days. The top level takes about a year.
    • A level only ever goes up. Switching to another companion keeps the level you reached.
    • Your weight, your loads and how you look never change it. Only how often you train does.
    • It has a mood. After a break it gets sleepy, but it never scolds you. One workout wakes it up.
    • It speaks in a small speech cloud when something happens, like a new record. Tap it to hear more.

  • Backups and export

    Back up, restore, and take your log with you.

    In detail
    • The app backs itself up a few seconds after you log or change a set. It keeps the last 7 backups on your phone.
    • Tap Back up now in Settings whenever you like.
    • Restore puts a backup back. It saves a rescue copy of what was there first.
    • Export a spreadsheet of everything you have logged. Email it, or save it to Files.
    • Import a spreadsheet the app made, on this phone or a new one. It adds what is new and changes nothing else.
    • Connect Apple Health if you want to, and disconnect it at any time.

  • Reminders and extras

    Celebrations, optional reminders and a glossary.

    In detail
    • A small celebration when you set a personal record or reach a streak milestone.
    • Seasonal looks for nine festivals in the year, such as Halloween, Diwali and Christmas. Only for the ones you tick.
    • Optional reminders for a weekly weigh-in, the weekly check-in, and a long break from training.
    • A built-in glossary explains every term the app uses, in plain words.
    • It all works without internet.

Who GymTracker is for

A good fit if you

  • want a workout log that stays private
  • lift weights, do cardio, or both
  • train at a gym, at home, or with just your body

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. You open the app and start. There is nothing to sign up for, and we never ask for your email.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone. We have no server, so your data never reaches us. It only leaves your phone if you send a file yourself, or if you turn on crash reports. Crash reports never include your training data.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline, including the exercise videos.

Does it use Apple Health?

Only if you connect it. It can then read your weight, a few body details and cardio from other apps. It can also add your workouts and weigh-ins to Health. You choose what it may read, and you can disconnect at any time.

What if I lose my phone, or get a new one?

We cannot get your data back for you, because we never have it. Your iPhone’s own backup includes the app’s data. To keep a copy somewhere else, export a spreadsheet. On a new phone, import that spreadsheet to bring your log back.

Is it medical advice?

No. GymTracker is for general fitness. If you have a health condition or an injury, talk to a doctor before you start a new exercise plan.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. An Android version is planned.

How much does it cost, and when is it out?

The app is still in development. Pricing will be announced before launch. Email us and we will tell you when it is on the App Store.

How do I get help?

Email support@kalikoutsi.com. Inside the app, the glossary explains every term in plain words.
