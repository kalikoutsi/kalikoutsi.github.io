GymTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy covers the GymTracker iOS app, published by Kalikoutsi. The companion Terms of Use cover what the app does and does not promise.

The short version: GymTracker has no account, no server, and nothing to sign up for. Everything you record lives on your phone, and the only thing that can ever leave it is a crash report you have explicitly switched on, or a file you have explicitly asked to export.

What stays on your device

Your workouts, sets, reps and loads; your exercise history; your body weight and other body metrics; your goals, programs and calendar; your avatar and awards; and every setting you choose. All of it is stored in a database on the device itself. We operate no server that receives it, we cannot see it, and we have no way to retrieve it — which also means we cannot recover it for you if you lose the device without a backup.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. GymTracker requires no account, no email address and no sign-in. We do not know how many people use the app, who they are, or what they do in it.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no tracking pixels and no third-party marketing tools. Nothing you do in the app is measured or profiled, and nothing about you is shared with data brokers or advertisers. In App Store terms, the app collects no data used to track you.

Crash reporting, which is off unless you turn it on

GymTracker can send crash reports so that faults can be fixed. It is off by default and becomes active only if you switch it on yourself in Settings. You can switch it off again at any time.

When it is on, a crash sends the stack trace, your device model, and the OS and app version to Sentry, hosted in the European Union. It does not include your name, email, IP address or any account identifier — there is no account — and it carries no workout data and no body metrics. There is no performance tracing, no session replay and no usage analytics: a crash is the only event that causes a request.

The app also keeps a crash log on the device itself, to show you what went wrong. That log never leaves your phone, whatever the reporting setting says.

Backups and exports

GymTracker can write a backup file, and can export your training history as a spreadsheet. Both happen only when you ask for them. The app hands the file to your device's own share sheet and you choose where it goes — Files, Mail, AirDrop or anywhere else iOS offers. We operate no cloud storage and the file is never uploaded to us. Once you have sent it somewhere, what happens to it is governed by whichever service you sent it to, not by this policy.

Notifications

Reminders — rest timers, weigh-ins, check-ins and inactivity reminders — are scheduled on your device by the app itself. They do not travel through a push notification server, so we do not know whether one was sent, received or opened.

Health data

GymTracker never touches Apple Health until you connect it in Settings, and you can disconnect at any time. When connected, it reads your weight, height, date of birth and sex, and your workouts, and it writes the workouts and weigh-ins you record in GymTracker back to Health. Readings taken from Health are shown in the app but are not kept in the app's own database, its backups or its spreadsheet exports; only what you type into GymTracker is stored. Nothing from Health ever leaves your phone through GymTracker.

Your rights over your data

Because your data never reaches us, you exercise these rights directly in the app rather than by writing to us:

  • Access and portability — Settings › Data › Export to spreadsheet gives you everything the app holds, in a format other software can open.
  • Erasure — Settings › Data › Reset deletes it. Deleting the app deletes it too.
  • Rectification — every value you have entered can be edited in the app.

If you have questions we can actually answer, write to gymtracker_support@kalikoutsi.com. Please note that we cannot look up, restore or delete your data for you, because we do not have it.

Children

GymTracker is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age, beyond the opt-in crash reports described above. Where the app gives guidance about eating that depends on age, it applies more cautious limits for anyone under eighteen.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
